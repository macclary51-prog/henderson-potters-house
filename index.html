Welcome to The Henderson Potter's House. Join us for worship, fellowship, prayer, and messages centered on faith and everyday life.

The Potter's House is a life-giving Christian church serving the Henderson community. We believe in the life-changing power available through an active relationship with Jesus. God has a unique purpose for each individual, and we invite you to come discover His plan for your life!
